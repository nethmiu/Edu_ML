Title: Microservices Architecture: A Distributed Approach to Building Applications

Abstract:

This paper presents microservices architecture as a design approach for building complex applications as a collection of small, independent services. Each service operates in its own process and communicates with lightweight mechanisms, typically via HTTP APIs. The microservices are designed around business capabilities and can be independently deployed using automated deployment machinery. Unlike monolithic architectures, microservices exhibit minimal centralized management, allowing for increased flexibility and scalability. Furthermore, this approach enables the use of diverse programming languages and data storage technologies, fostering a more agile development process. This abstract highlights the key principles and benefits of microservices architecture, providing a foundational understanding of its application in modern software development.
