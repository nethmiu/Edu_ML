This abstract presents a comprehensive overview of microservices architecture, a design paradigm for building complex applications as a constellation of loosely coupled services. Each service operates autonomously, utilizing lightweight communication mechanisms, such as HTTP-based APIs. This approach enables independent deployment and scalability, facilitated by automated machinery. Furthermore, microservices are designed around distinct business capabilities, allowing for technological heterogeneity in terms of programming languages and data storage solutions. As a result, centralized management is minimized, fostering greater flexibility and adaptability within the system.
